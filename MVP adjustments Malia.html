How it works

Two short paths that meet in the middle

Both sides sign up with the same short questionnaire. A person on our team reads both and suggests a match only once the checks are done.

FAQ

Questions people ask

The worries that came up most when we talked to students and homeowners.

Can I sign up without using the website?

Yes. Request a call and a person on our team fills in the sign-up and questionnaire with you by phone. Many hosts prefer that.

Why do I need an account to see rooms?

Listings describe real homes and requests describe real people's plans. Keeping them for signed-in members protects both. Signing up is free: an account and a 2-minute questionnaire.

What does TwoGen cost?

Joining, browsing, the questionnaire and intros are free. Both sides pay the same: $60 once for ID and background checks, then 4% of the monthly rent each while the stay runs. At $950 rent, the student pays $988 and the host receives $912. Nothing is charged until you and a match both say yes after a video call.

When do the checks happen?

After the video call, once you and your match both want to go ahead. You confirm your identity, complete a background check and two people give a reference. Nobody meets in person before both sides have passed. This keeps everyone safe and keeps the service affordable.

How is rent paid?

The student pays rent plus 4% to TwoGen each month. We pass the rent on to the host minus their 4%, so neither side has to chase money.

Is the student expected to be a caregiver?

No. Help is ordinary household help like cooking together, garden work or shoveling snow, capped at 3 hours a week in the agreement. Personal or medical care is never part of a TwoGen stay.

Is the household help a job?

It is agreed help between housemates, not paid work, and it is capped in writing. Because rules on this vary, a real launch would have a local attorney review the agreement first.

What if we do not get along?

Either side can raise it at a check-in, ask for a mediator, or give 30 days' written notice. If safety is at risk, the stay can end sooner. The host's monthly fee stops the day the stay ends.

How do you decide who to match?

Both sides fill in the same questionnaire. We weigh each answer by how much it matters to both sides, rule out pairs with deal-breakers, allergy conflicts or missing must-haves, and check budget, stay length and commute. A person on our team then reviews the best fits before anyone is introduced.

Who can be a lodger?

Anyone 18 or older with a verifiable reason to be in Bozeman: MSU students, international newcomers, new hires and interns.

Who can see my personal information?

Listings show a general area, a nickname and room details. Requests show a first initial and plans. Names, emails and addresses are seen only by the TwoGen team. A match learns your first name after you both agree to meet.

Do I need my landlord's or insurer's permission to host?

If you rent, you need written permission from your landlord. Homeowners should tell their insurer they are renting a room. Both are on the host checklist.
